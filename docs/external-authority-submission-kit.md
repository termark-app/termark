## Directory submission priorities

### AlternativeTo or comparable curated software directories

Use when the platform accepts commercial desktop/mobile software and permits an official website plus GitHub/documentation evidence. Submit SSH/SFTP client categories and only comparisons that users can verify. Avoid self-authored ratings or unsupported “best” claims.

### Product Hunt or comparable launch directories

Use only when an owner-controlled account can answer comments and maintain the listing. Prepare original screenshots supplied by the owner, a concise product description, current platform availability, and a real launch/support plan. Do not launch merely to create a backlink.

## Developer community article briefs

Publish only from a real owner/author account. Each article should solve a technical problem independently, disclose Termark where relevant, link to primary evidence, and avoid becoming a disguised product pitch.

1. SSH jump hosts: explain ProxyJump concepts, multi-hop troubleshooting, and port-forwarding perspective.
2. Local/remote/dynamic SSH forwarding: provide threat boundaries and reproducible examples.
3. SFTP workflow: compare CLI scp/sftp with dual-pane workflows and remote editing.
4. AI terminal safety: explain context use, explicit confirmation, and why generated commands still require review.
5. Cross-platform SSH configuration: discuss portable asset organization without inventing benchmark results.

Suitable channels depend on accounts the owner actually controls: DEV Community, Medium, Zhihu, Juejin, SegmentFault, or a personal engineering blog. Do not automate account creation, CAPTCHA, or mass cross-posting. Canonicalize or substantially adapt cross-posted articles where the platform supports it.

## Independent tutorial / review outreach

A third-party source must remain editorially independent. Offer factual access and an evidence pack, not prescribed praise. Ask reviewers to test concrete workflows:

- connect through a jump host;
- open SFTP beside a terminal;
- configure one forwarding rule;
- inspect credential and sync boundaries;
- ask AI to explain an error and verify that execution requires confirmation.

Never pay for undisclosed positive reviews. Sponsored access or compensation must be disclosed according to the publisher’s rules.
